Travel Philosophy

I believe travel is about more than just seeing new places. It's about:

  • Learning - Every place has something to teach you
  • Perspective - Seeing how others live changes how you see your own life
  • Adventure - Stepping outside your comfort zone
  • Connection - Meeting people and understanding different ways of life
